Invoice Amount — by status and office

Full invoiced amount (Net Product Value + Freight/S&H + Tariff), for the date range and Ship_Date window selected above. Shipped = actually shipped (linked Sales Order Status = Shipped), regardless of payment status. Scheduled = not yet shipped but has an ESD (Ship_Date) on file. Forecast = not yet shipped, no ESD yet, and the invoice is no older than 3 months (older = treated as effectively dead/cancelled).
