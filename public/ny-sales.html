Conversion rate — quote to order, order to shipped

How to read this chart:
• Order→Shipped should stay well above Quote→Order year-round - fulfillment is a near-100% commitment on already-won orders, while win rate is naturally much lower.
• If the two lines converge or cross, treat it as a warning sign - either fulfillment is lagging, or win rate looks unusually high and is worth checking.
• Monthly spikes/dips are normal timing noise - judge this pattern over a full year, not month to month.
